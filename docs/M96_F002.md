# M96-F002 — small Fillet on long offset walls

Classification: `DEFECT`. Owner: `geosolve-sketch-features` root polishing. Reproduced
from the M96 silicone groove at baseline `a918716` with the new channel composition.
No solver equation or residual changed.

The 3.8 mm inner Fillet joins the horizontal wall `[-112.8,-48.8] → [104.8,-48.8]`
and vertical wall `[104.8,-48.8] → [104.8,48.8]`, at model scale 1. The public
`ComputedFeatureAuthoringSnapshot::resolve_fillet_corner` returned `NoLocalRoot`;
the application reported `SingularFillet`. Its valid center is `[101,-45]`.
Evaluating the horizontal contact at `1 - 3.8 / 217.6` produces
`100.99999999999999`, leaving a representable center mismatch of about `1.42e-14`.
The fixed `1e-14` polishing target therefore requested further reduction that
floating-point contact parameters could not represent.

Ordinary root iterations and results remain unchanged. Only a stalled Newton/line
search may finish within eight machine epsilons of the evaluated coordinate and
local derivative magnitude. This returns a candidate root; independent arc,
contact, radius, transversality and branch validation still run with their
unchanged acceptance thresholds. Parallel parents and out-of-span roots still fail.

The exact owning regression failed before repair. It now checks nine combinations
of scales `1`, `1e-6`, `1e6` and translations `[0,0]`, `[300,-200]`, `[-300,200]`,
independent center/radius/tangency invariants, explicit side/endpoint state,
persisted feature evaluation, and unchanged source authority. A second regression
checks parallel parents and a radius whose contacts lie outside both supports.
