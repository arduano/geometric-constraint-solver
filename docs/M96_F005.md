# M96-F005: channel boundary validation used design seeds after a dimension edit

The manifold reservoir-width edit from 60 to 62 mm failed publication with
`upperChannel` reporting 18 open ends instead of 2. The native solve and computed
Fillets had moved together, but channel validation evaluated native fragments
against retained design coordinates. Those coordinates remain initialization
seeds; the computed contacts belong to the current independently accepted solve.

This is a sketch-code composition `DEFECT`, reproduced during M96 on base revision
`a9187164ecb5f46f04c940c0cd49740659a710b0` with the active M96 working changes.
The exact original failure is captured in `target/m96/manifold-edits.log`.

The smaller public reproduction is a fixed-origin L polyline
`[0,0] → [30,0] → [30,30]`, horizontal/vertical constraints, two driving 30 mm
lengths, and a 6 mm channel with centreline bend radius 5 mm and an end cap.
Editing only the first length to 32 mm leaves the managed point seeds unchanged.
Before the fix, incremental composition reported 10 open ends instead of 2 in
2.18 seconds. The fixture source and both compiled envelopes are retained under
`packages/geosolve-sketch-code/test/fixtures/managed-channel-boundary-driven*`:

`composition/channel_validation.rs` now obtains native geometry from
`accepted_state_for_current_input().document()`, matching the authority consumed
by computed-feature evaluation. A missing current accepted state rejects. No
topology assertion, tolerance, branch, equation or solver behavior changed.

The owning regression
`channel_dimension_edit_qualifies_solved_boundaries_instead_of_design_seeds`
checks incremental edit, cold replay and serialized native restoration. It
independently checks finite hard residuals at most `1e-9`, zero remaining DOF,
the moved endpoint `[32,30]`, R2/R8 Fillets at `[27,5]`, and the deliberate
difference between accepted geometry and retained design seeds. The existing
crossing-cap rejection and transactional-retention regression still passes.
