# M87 — Cohesive managed parameters and browser-free design loop

Status: **OPEN. M87-F003 sample-authority repair is proportionally mechanically qualified;
M87-U9/U10 focused human visual UAT and milestone closure remain pending**. The sound managed-
control, headless-authoring, routing-board, twelve-demo CNC/Gridfinity,
full-detail retained-camera and graphics-audit work is retained; the experimental adaptive-detail/
LOD prototype remains deleted. The supervising user's earlier scoped disposition remains historical
acceptance of U1-U8 without claiming a separate row-by-row replay. No clean exact-source/tree
nomination, immutable freeze, public deployment or service retirement is claimed; the mutable
Tailscale listener remains development infrastructure. `docs/M87_AUDIT.md` owns the audited boundary
and ADR 0042 owns the architecture decision.

## Required behavior

1. Repair M87-F001's reproduced Typed Panel generated-Fillet radius rollback at the smallest public
   code/workbench owner. Change no solver or Fillet mathematics.
2. Publish a transient, bounded managed-control manifest derived from authenticated source spans,
   direct declaration schemas, artifact bindings, and accepted semantic consumers.
3. Cover every current declaration-backed explicit non-DoF definition literal: numeric/unit,
   integer, boolean, closed enum, constraint/dimension state, branch/orientation, and feature
   parameters. Keep helper-level validation and formatting for Natural and Text schemas without
   claiming a real declaration or managed-only control fixture for either kind. Point/solver-
   instance fields remain on existing drafts and never write solved values to code.
4. Make `EditLens` a compatible presentation hint only. References, identity and structure remain
   read-only or navigate to their source owner. An absent member has no parser span and produces no
   fabricated row; an explicit `null` has a parser span and produces one read-only `Null` row.
5. Apply exact-CAS control batches through one parse/expand/reconcile/materialize/independent-
   validation transaction and one outer history entry. Retained failures preserve accepted scene.
6. Make shared fan-out explicit. Either Typed Panel Fillet edits its one `mm(4)` input and updates
   both Fillets; repeated custom-patch invocations never route to the first module use.
7. Decorate the Inspector and Code panel from the same control data. All managed parameters remain
   reachable from the Code panel even when no exact selection/property inverse exists.
8. Route shared Fillet radius grips through a presentation-independent grouped preview and the
   same source transaction on release. Point dragging/overlays remain unchanged.
9. Add a strict code-control RPC for inspect/edit/outer Undo/Redo. Code-mode mutating Intent RPC
   rejects rather than bypassing outer authority.
10. Extract shared camera/SVG presentation into `geosolve-sketch-render` and add native-only
    pure-Rust PNG with pinned bundled fonts and no system/external resources.
11. Add `geosolve-headless` Rust APIs and CLI commands `inspect`, `render`, and exact-CAS `edit` for
    managed source, canonical projects, and bundled demos, with structured deterministic reports
    and atomic non-overwriting output directories.
12. Keep browser navigation live without weakening accepted-scene authority: a transient retained-
    presentation failure must reconcile exactly and preserve future frames. Every camera frame
    keeps the complete scene paint; adaptive or reduced-detail rendering is outside M87.
13. Dogfood those seams in one tenth bundled project: a robotic cable-harness routing board with
    eight keyed open routes, shared/local clip and bend controls, adaptive generated clips/Fillets,
    one solver-instance service-loop drag, keyed insertion, deterministic headless products and no
    route-specific solver, editor, renderer or TypeScript runtime.
14. Expand the catalog from ten to twelve with a CNC joinery fit coupon. Preserve one 120 x 140 mm
    female blank, loose/nominal/press 70 mm-wide mortises at respective heights
    18.4/18.0/17.6 mm, three 95 x 18 mm tabs,
    twelve shared 3.175 mm cutter-radius relief circles and ten shared 6 mm handling Fillets. Prove
    invocation locality and keyed removal/reinsertion generations without a coupon-specific engine.
15. Add a plain Gridfinity 1 x 1 x 3U central material section as one symmetric closed 26-point
    contour: 41.5 mm outer width, 35.6 mm base bottom, 4.75 mm staged base rising to 7 mm, 21 mm
    body, 0.95 mm walls and 4.4 mm nominal lip. Keep the two 2.8 mm floor Fillets independent of
    the two 0.6 mm lip Fillets.
16. Treat both additions strictly as 2D/2.5D manufacturing-intent sketches. Do not claim CAM,
    toolpath, cutter compensation, boolean, solid or production-manufacturing authority.
17. Repair M87-F003 without changing nominal sample geometry. Fully constrain CNC from exactly one
    absolute point plus relational placement datums, and fully constrain Gridfinity from exactly
    one scalar Y datum, live left/right symmetry and dimensioned diagonal projections. Both must
    report numerical/structural nullity zero and equality/bidirectional bounded DOF zero.

## Non-goals

No new primitive/constraint/dimension, equation, Jacobian, residual, priority, tolerance, branch
inference, general expression inversion, custom-TypeScript runtime, point-source write-back,
browser/server dependency, linkage renderer, stateful agent service, route solver, route-specific
renderer, CAM, toolpath, automatic cutter compensation, boolean/solid authority, or PNG
mathematical oracle. The observed performance work is deferred to a follow-up milestone; this
amendment does not activate M88.
